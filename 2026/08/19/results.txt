The table-free path computes digit placement from the delimiter positions
(vpcompressb of [0..15] by the marker mask) instead of loading a precomputed
shuffle. It is correct -- both fast paths accept exactly the same strings with
the same value -- but on Golden Cove it loses ~6 cycles: vpcompressb is a
multi-uop, ~6-cycle instruction at the head of a five-op index chain, whereas
the table lookup is a fully pipelined L1 hit behind imul/shr.
